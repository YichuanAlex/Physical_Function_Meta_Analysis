# Trim-and-Fill Analysis: TUG

## Figure Description

This trim-and-fill analysis adjusts for publication bias by trimming asymmetrically distributed studies and imputing their symmetric counterparts. The funnel plot shows observed studies and the expected funnel shape to assess symmetry.

## Methods

### Statistical Analysis
- **Meta-analysis model**: Random Effects Model (DerSimonian-Laird)
- **Effect measure**: Mean Difference (MD)
- **Confidence intervals**: 95%
- **Publication bias assessment**: Visual inspection of funnel plots
- **Fail-Safe N**: Rosenthal's method

### Visualization
- **Color scheme**: Colorblind-friendly palette (seaborn.colorblind)
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI
- **Format**: PNG
- **Axes**: Clear labeling with appropriate titles
- **Annotations**: Summary statistics and methodological details
